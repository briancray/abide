A `GET`'s `throttle` or `debounce` reaches its browser copy: the build writes it into the wrapper beside `key`, `tags` and `identity`, and the copy starts the keys a page reaches at that pace, as a `memo` does — so `reactive.derived(() => searchDeals({ q }))` over a handler declared `{ debounce: 300 }` sends one request once the typing stops, with no `memo` between them. A pace the build cannot read is warned on (16.110, 18.4, 18.15).
