A press on a `combobox`'s field opens its list, listing every option, and a second press shuts it, as a press on a `menu`'s trigger does; a `search` combobox opens on a press once its field holds text. Focus arriving by Tab still opens nothing. Alt+ArrowDown opens the list and Alt+ArrowUp shuts it, without stepping the active option (RULEBOOK 51.99, 51.100, 51.14).
