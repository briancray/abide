`CommandPalette` is a modal search over what an app can do: it opens on a key (`mod+k` by default) or a bound `open`, lists the commands whose label or keywords hold every word typed, moves with the arrows, runs a command on Enter or a click once it has shut, and leaves Escape to its dialog, which hands focus back (RULEBOOK 51.72-51.78).
