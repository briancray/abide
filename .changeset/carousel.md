`carousel(options)` is a scroll-snap carousel as parts — `host`, `item(item)`, `previous` and `next` — that rests on the item `selected` names, follows the item a scroll centers as `centered`, and scrolls with `scrollTo(key)` and back with `reset()`. Where the browser reports `scrollsnapchanging` and `scrollsnapchange` they set `centered`, and where it does not a scroll is measured once a frame (RULEBOOK 53.64-53.73).
