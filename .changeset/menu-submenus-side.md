`menu` opens submenus: a menu whose trigger is another menu's item is its submenu, a pick in it closing every menu. A menu given `side: 'right'` or `'left'` opens on the arrow toward that side and closes on the arrow back, so a vertical `toolbar({ role: 'menubar' })` opens its menus to the right.
