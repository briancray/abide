`panZoom`, a surface moved and magnified: spread `surface` on the element that clips and `content` on what moves inside it. A wheel pans, and with Ctrl or ⌘ — or a trackpad's pinch — zooms about the pointer; a drag pans, two pointers pinch, a double click zooms in, and the keys pan, zoom and go home (53.84-53.88, 53.90). `fit(box, margin)` frames a region of the content, and the magnification keeps to `min` and `max` (53.87, 53.89).
