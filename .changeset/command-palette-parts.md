`commandPalette(options)` is the command palette as parts an app spreads onto its own `Dialog` and list — `dialog`, `input`, `listbox`, `option(command)`, `empty`, `results()` and `open` — listening for its `hotkey` on the window itself, and `CommandPalette` is now default markup over those parts, behaving as it did (RULEBOOK 51.65-51.71).
