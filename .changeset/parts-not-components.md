abide ships no components. Every `@abide/abide/<Name>.abide` entry is gone — `Dialog`, `Prompts`, `Toasts`, `Tooltips`, `Announcer`, `Combobox`, `CommandPalette`, `Range`, `SortableTable`, `Chart` with `Line`, `Column` and `Area`, `Pie`, `Sparkline`, `Markdown`, `Chat`, `Time` and `Pairing` — and each widget is the parts function it was markup over, spread on markup the app writes. `prompts`, `toasts` and `announce` import from `@abide/abide`, shown where the root layout spreads `prompts.host()`, `toasts.host()` and `announce.host()` (and `tooltip.host()` for hints). A chart hands `chart` its `series` directly. What the components did beyond their parts moved into them: `chat(options)` is a conversation over an assistant handler as parts — the transcript, each turn and piece, and the composer that sends, queues, stops and takes images; `markdown.incremental(options)` is a document as sections that keep their identity, and so their nodes, while a stream grows it, a fenced block named in `blocks` handed back for the app to render; and `pairing(options)`'s `step` is now where the pairing stands in full — `connecting`, `slow` after `slowAfter`, `offline`, `unavailable`, `click`, `waiting`, `install` or `paired` (RULEBOOK 49, 52.17, 52.18, 52.43, 53.31-53.49).
