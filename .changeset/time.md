`Time` writes how long ago or how soon a time is, as `format.relative` does, in a `<time>` with its `datetime` and the full date as its `title`, and keeps it current: one clock ticks every `Time` on the page at the moment the soonest label next reads differently, sets no timer while the page is hidden or no `Time` stands, and a tick writes only the labels that turned (RULEBOOK 52.72-52.77).
