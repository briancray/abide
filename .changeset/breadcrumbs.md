`breadcrumbs({ trail, href, max })`, a trail of links to the page, the last carrying `aria-current="page"`. Past `max`, `items` keeps the first crumb and the last `max - 1` with an `overflow` between, and the overflow is a `menu`'s parts, the `folded` crumbs its items (51.136-51.140).
