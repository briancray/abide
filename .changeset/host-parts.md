`toasts.host()` and `prompts.host()` are the notices' live region and the prompts' dialog as parts, called from the script of the one component that shows them and spread onto markup of your own: the region, each notice, its action and close button; the dialog, its form, the field, the error and the two buttons. `Toasts` and `Prompts` are default markup over them (RULEBOOK 50.6, 50.10, 50.17, 50.34, 50.36).
