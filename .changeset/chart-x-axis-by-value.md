`chart` takes `xAxis: 'values'`, standing its x ticks at round values across the x range rather than at the positions, and `xZero`, taking zero into the x range with a line down the plot there as `plot.xZero`. Every x tick now carries its `value` and a `grid` line down the plot, a tick at a round value an `index` of `undefined`, and the y gridlines and zero line carry `data-axis="y"` (RULEBOOK 52.25, 52.120, 52.121).
