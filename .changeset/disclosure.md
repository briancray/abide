`disclosure`, `accordion` and `clamp`, parts functions for showing and hiding. `disclosure()` is a button and the region it shows, the region hidden `until-found` so find in page still reaches it and opens it (51.116-51.119). `accordion()` is a `disclosure` per key, one open at a time or, with `multiple: true`, any number (51.120, 51.121). `clamp({ lines })` cuts text to a few lines and says whether there is more as `overflowing`, its button a disclosure's over the text (51.122-51.124).
