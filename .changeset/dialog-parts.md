`dialog(options)` is a native modal as a part spread onto a `<dialog>`: shown and closed by `open` both ways, Escape and the backdrop held back where it is not `dismissible`, `onclose` handed the `returnValue`. `Dialog` is default markup over it, and `commandPalette`'s `dialog` part is now `dialog`'s, spread onto a plain `<dialog>` rather than a `Dialog` (RULEBOOK 50.1-50.4, 51.65).
