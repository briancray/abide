`markdown` and `Markdown` take `blocks`: a fenced block whose language is one of its names renders as the caller's markup (`markdown`) or component (`Markdown`), handed its source and the rest of its info — a chart, a callout, a code block with a Copy button. `href` may answer the address with the `target` and `rel` it opens with, and a reference link (`[text][label]`, `[text][]`, `[label]`) takes its address from a definition anywhere in the source (RULEBOOK 52.40-52.43)
