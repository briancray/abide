BREAKING: an app is laid out by subject. Every directory under the root is a segment of an address, and a `page.abide`, `layout.abide` or `error.abide` answers at its directory's path — `src/billing/page.abide` is `/billing`; a `server` or `public` directory is never a segment. Handlers are exported from a `<script server>` block in any `.abide` outside those two, and answer at the directory's route plus the export's name — `.ts` handler modules and `src/server/app.ts` are gone, and the root `layout.abide`'s server block holds the app's hooks. `middleware` and `onError` are scoped to their file's route, `onConfig` and `onPrincipal` compose, path params merge into a handler's args, a `server/` directory is fenced from the browser, `public/` directories mount anywhere, and the one import alias is `#src/*`. The desktop launcher's files leave `src/bundle/`: `src/launcher.abide`, beside the root layout, exports its options as a typed `launcher` from its `<script module>`, read as the app launches (no more `launcher.json`, and the app is named by its package), and the icon is the public file at `/icon.png` or `/icon.icns`. The built-in launcher is the icon, the name and a Start and a Connect… button, each opening its form in a dialog, colored by the most saturated hue of the icon.
