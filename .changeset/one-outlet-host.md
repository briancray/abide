`Toasts` and `Prompts` show in the last of each standing, as `Tooltips` already did: a second one a nested layout renders shows nothing, rather than every notice twice or a second modal over the first. A notice or a prompt asked for with none rendered now warns once on `abide:toasts` or `abide:prompts`, as a hint does on `abide:tooltip`, rather than being lost without a word (RULEBOOK 50.36).
