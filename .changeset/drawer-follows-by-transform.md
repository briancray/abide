A `drawer` given `stops` follows a drag by a `transform` alone, carrying `data-following`, and is sized once it is let go: the drag is stood at its largest stop once and lays nothing out per move (50.63). A tap on its handle, a press let go within 8 pixels, stands it at the next stop, going round (50.84).
