`time(options)` is a time kept current as parts: `seen.time(at)` spread onto a `<time>` of your own carries its `datetime` and `title` and is followed by the page's one clock for as long as it is bound, and `seen.text(at)` is its label, so one set of parts serves every row of a list. `Time` is default markup over it (RULEBOOK 52.73, 52.74, 49.9).
