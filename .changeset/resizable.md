`resizable`, WAI-ARIA's window splitter as parts: spread `panel` on a panel and `handle` on its edge, and dragging the handle sizes the panel along `axis`, toward the `edge` it stands on (53.72, 53.73). The size keeps to `min` and `max`, and where `keep` is given to the parent's room less what stands beside it (53.74); the arrow keys of its axis step it, Home and End take it to its bounds, Enter collapses and restores it, and a double click gives it back its own size (53.75-53.77). One per axis spread on one grip sizes a box from its corner.
