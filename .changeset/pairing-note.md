An app can say why it asks to pair: `provider.claudeCode.bridgeCommand({ note })` and `provider.claudeCode.bridge({ note })`, or `ABIDE_BRIDGE_NOTE` for `pairing()`, keep a note with the pairing's code, and the question a pairing link asks shows it under its headline as the app's own words — `From CRM: …`, one line of at most 200 characters. The rest of the question stays the bridge's.
