`announce.host()` hands back the polite and the assertive live region as parts — `polite`, `assertive`, and `said(politeness)` for the message each holds — so an app can render the regions in markup of its own, as `toasts.host` and `prompts.host` already allow. `Announcer` is its default markup (RULEBOOK 50.51, 50.52).
