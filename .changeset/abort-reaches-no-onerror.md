An abort (a `DOMException` named `AbortError`: a superseded call's, a stopped stream's) reaches no `onError`, the browser's or the server's — 37.28, as 37.25 keeps an aborted call from `onRefusal`. The server still writes it to its log, and under `abide start` an abort nothing handled no longer stops the app (37.10).
