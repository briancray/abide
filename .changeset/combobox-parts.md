`combobox` is the field that lists what matches as parts an app spreads onto an input, a popup and options of its own, with its own option and chip markup: the combobox role and states, the keys, the search, the pick and the top-layer popup. `Combobox` is now default markup over those parts and behaves as before (RULEBOOK 51.13-51.33, 51.55, 51.56, 51.57).
