The question a pairing link asks is each desktop's own, by the asking app: on macOS a native `NSAlert` the bridge shows itself, in front, with the site's `/icon.png` as its icon; on Windows a dialog with a bold headline, the icon and labelled buttons in place of Yes/No/Cancel; on Linux zenity or kdialog titled by the app, with its icon. The headline names the app beside its site — `CRM (crm.example)` — which `/__abide/bridge`'s holder answer now carries as `name`.
